## Everything else

There are no other third-party assets. Every shape is drawn from primitives
(`scripts/fx/shapes.gd`, `scripts/fx/palette.gd`) and every sound effect is
synthesised at load (`scripts/fx/sfx_bank.gd`) — no sprites, no sample packs,
no fonts beyond the engine default.

Built with [Godot 4.7](https://godotengine.org/).
